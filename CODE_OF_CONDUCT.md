# Community conduct

KYRIN is a personal portfolio project. Participants should be able to discuss
its code and limitations without harassment or disclosure of private data.
This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests and review discussions.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an implementation or argument, not a
  person's identity, background, language or experience.
- Welcome questions and explain disagreements constructively.
- Do not post harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual content,
  spam or another person's private information.
- Do not publish credentials, private recordings or personal screenshots.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions,
close contributions or block repeated abuse. Responses should be proportionate
to the behavior and protect the people involved.

For an ordinary public discussion problem, notify the maintainer in the
relevant thread without repeating abusive or private material. If a report
needs private handling, ask for a private contact route without posting the
sensitive details. GitHub's own reporting tools are also available for abuse
or policy violations. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
